## In plain words

You want to see about 25 real belief tools before we design our own, and pick what you like from them. We have 7
(r1–r5, plus r2b and r4b, which still have to pass the quality gate). This round adds 18 more, from all kinds:
general methods, AI coaches, addiction tools, and tools for dating, social confidence, procrastination and phone use.

Each new tool gets the method that worked in round 2: one agent goes through the real tool like a new user, a
second one looks for what the first missed, then a spec, a working study copy, a review, and the quality gate.
Copies you can click through like the real thing, with the skip button. Tools that nobody can walk for free get
no copy: their card shows the real video clips and the screens that were really seen, so nothing is guessed.

A last pass gathers every belief and every belief answer from all 25 tools into one list, grouped by life area,
on its own page where you strike the ones you don't want.

## How others do this now (checked 2026-11-21)
Checked in search results today (summaries, pages not opened):
- Flow libraries let you step through a recorded flow at your own pace and show a "flow tree" of an app's main
  journeys; some record whole journeys as video instead of single screens.
- Grouping many statements with an AI: a 2026 study (28 card-sort studies, 1,399 people) found a model predicts the
  main groups people make but disagrees on where single items go; a 2026 lab tool keeps a person in the loop because
  the grouping is not transparent; a practitioner warns that the grouping must be agreed by people, not handed to AI.
From round 2's research (checked 2026-11-20, `RESEARCH-1b.md`): frames from walkthrough videos by scene change, a
spec before the build, a real-beside-copy picture loop, a separate judge.
From memory, not checked today: good teardowns tag every screen with what it demands of the user.

What we take over: a step strip and a jump list ("flow tree") outside each copy so you always know where you are;
real clips beside each copy; the AI groups beliefs into families but never drops or rewords one, and you make the

## PHASES
Models (memory `big-plans-small-builds`): specs and the gate judge on Opus; walks, gap routes, builds, reviews, gate
scorers, collection and pages on Sonnet; mechanical checks on Haiku. Load: `tools/load.sh` before every batch:
ok → up to 5 agents at once, busy → 4, game → 1. One emulator, one agent at a time; it is stopped after each use.
The executor runs units on a rolling queue (a unit moves to its next step as soon as an agent is free) and keeps
`round3/STATUS.md` so a crash or a context reset loses nothing. Long work lives in `~/.cache`, never `/tmp`.

### Phase 0: set-up (Sonnet executor) — depends on nothing
Throwaway inboxes for the 9 units that need one, the status file, a check that every brief exists.

### Phase 1: finish round 2's last two copies (Sonnet builders, gate C on Opus) — runs beside Phase 2
r2b and r4b each get one fix round from their round-3 judge notes (r2b: one dead tap; r4b: bottom buttons fall off
shorter phone screens) and one more gate round. Pass → look page; fail → "not ready".

### Phase 2: walk the real tools (Sonnet, one agent per tool)
Terms read first. Then the real tool as a new user at phone size, as far as its row in `UNITS-r3.md` says: always
the whole belief part, every answer path, the ending, the result and every email (inboxes polled daily for 7 days),
the first paywall (shot only), one return visit. AI coaches: the same three scripted belief chats in each
(nicotine, dating, productivity). Up to three public screen recordings per tool, with short clips of the belief
step for the look page. Every word of every belief and answer is kept in full.

### Phase 3: gap audit and one more route per gap (Sonnet, a different agent from the walker)
### Phase 4: specs (Opus), build + review + fix (Sonnet)
Spec adds "what really happens" in six sentences and the main path. If more than a quarter of the main path, or
the belief step itself, would be guessed, no spec is written and the tool moves to "seen on video". Copies get the
skip button, the step strip and the jump list, all outside the copied app.
Tools seen only on video (r11, r22, r23, and any tool moved there): a screen strip of the real screens in order,
3–6 real clips of the belief step, and what really happens in plain sentences, each with its source.
r3 is rebuilt from what was seen (D2); r1, r2, r4, r5 get the study chrome only (D3).

### Phase 5: quality gate (Sonnet scorers, Opus judge; at most 3 rounds)
Round 2's ten "hollow" checks, plus three new ones from your r3 note: a first-time viewer who writes down what
really happens must match the spec's account and must not get lost on the main path; too much guessing moves the
tool to "seen on video"; the skip button and step strip must work from the first screen to the end without typing.

### Phase 6: the belief list and your look (Sonnet; Haiku checks)
- Belief list: every belief, every answer (rebuttal, reframe, dispute, turnaround), every quiz ending, result and
  email, word for word, tagged by life area and source, from all 25 tools plus public myth pages for gambling,
  alcohol and dating. The AI groups same-meaning beliefs into families and never drops or rewords one; a check
  confirms every item landed once. A new private page per area: tap ✕ to strike, 👍 to keep, notes that save as you
  type.
- Look page: the same page as rounds 1–2 (your notes stay under their cards), grouped "General tools", "Tools for
  one area" and "Seen only on video", each card with the real name, what it is, what really happens in one
  sentence, the copy, real screens beside it, real clips, 👍 and a note box. It is updated whenever five more tools
  pass, so you can start before the whole run ends.
