# Role model candidates — long list

Written 2026-11-09 for `Plans/role-models.md`. **Everything here is a first guess and unchecked.** The "why"
lines and screen notes come from general knowledge. Phase 2's checking method decides every value, every
quote and every inclusion. A "check" note names what the screen step must look at; it is not a finding.

Columns:
- **Areas**: life plan area ids without the `lm_` prefix (health, fitness, mindset, emotions, relationship,
  mission, money, family, friends, fun, contribution, spirituality).
- **Screen**: `ok` (nothing known against him), `check: …` (something to examine and, if true, state),
  `shadow: …` (known dark side; he is in, and his profile states it plainly), `owner decides (…)` (an O4 or
  sexual-violence case; it goes on the decision page and stays hidden until the owner marks it).
- The owner's rule (2026-11-09): represent reality; people on the edge are more interesting. Nobody here is
  excluded for being dark.
- **Face**: `paint` = died before 1956, so an archive or painted portrait is allowed; `emblem` = alive or died
  1956 or later (O6).
- **Wave**: `1` = Phase 3 (about 60 people), `2` = Phase 10. `(current)` = in today's library of 30.

Counts: 13 categories, 233 names; 6 are "owner decides" cases. James Bond and Don Draper are not listed: they are fictional (O5).
