# The Guided Build — teaching the whole method, with his own plan as the worked example

## Context

Two things prompted this. First: the reasons drill exposed a pattern — the product ships *fields* for the framework but not the *method* behind them, so a user faces a blank box where he'd have run an exercise. Second: I claimed he never talks about approaching. That was wrong, and the way it was wrong matters — I searched the **curated** corpus (480 entries extracted for 12 narrow buckets) and treated absence there as absence in the source.

So this pass went to the source. 372 transcripts (2.38M words) were already on disk; **230 had never been mined.** Eight agents mined the gaps and everything they found was run through the existing 5-gram verbatim verifier.

**Corpus: 480 → 1,216 verified entries** (161 rejected as not-verbatim — the gate working). 180 videos cited, 736 of the entries new.

### The correction, at source

He was a professional cold-approach dating coach.

> *"when I was 21 my first business was called lifestyle transformations — this was a dating coaching business for men... I want to help other men overcome their fear of approaching women, improve their confidence... the dating mastery seminars"* — `Wr2SPFgW8iY`

> *"I used to count every single one and I counted it was over 2000 — 2000 groups of women, some individuals some groups, 2,000 in a year... I did approach [totals] over 10,000 for sure"* — `Wr2SPFgW8iY`

Both verified by direct read. The product's Dating area currently offers a couples-therapy toolkit (date-night, six-needs journal, marriage books) to a user whose goal is "get a girlfriend" — while the framework's own author has a complete, teachable approach curriculum we simply never extracted.

## Part 1 — What he teaches that we don't have

Ranked by how much it would change a real user's experience.

### 1. His order of operations is inverted from ours
He always starts **above** the areas and derives them; we start inside a room.

> *"having something like that is the most important first step when it comes to managing a life"* (whole-life vision) — `8kco2rjijjE`
> *"what is my ultimate vision for each of these areas **even before I get into the goals**"* — `8kco2rjijjE`

His sequence: state/solitude → whole-life vision → purpose → identity → code of conduct → derive areas → per-area vision/purpose/identity → rate 0-10 → 1yr → 90-day → monthly → per-goal purpose + action plan → rituals → weekly loop.

Ours: rooms → life-wide → commit. **Purpose lands after goals in ours and immediately after vision in his.** Rating comes before "your 10" in ours; he states the dependency outright: *"the step before this is you got to know what is your 10 in that area"* (`I-SoCQvNi9A`) — we fixed the beat order in v17, which turns out to match him.

### 2. No divergent phase before structure
He runs a **timed unfiltered brainstorm** (5-10 min per area), then numbers each line with a horizon (1/3/5/10/20), then **circles** the one-year set, then **80/20 twice** — once on goals, once on actions. Our flow goes straight to structured goals, so there is nothing to cut *from*.

### 3. The annual debrief is a prerequisite, and we have no such step
Good → challenges → lessons, worked from journals and camera roll, **good strictly first**, and a no-"but" rule: *"whenever you say that 'but' you're cheapening the accomplishment"* (`zuEb-1Ll2h8`).

### 4. Belief-change procedure — entirely absent from the product
We ask for an identity line with no method. He has a full one (`F4j974PvwSQ`): inventory via three stems (`I am ___` / `I can't ___` / `I don't have the ___`) → **adjudicate on usefulness, not truth** (*"the question is not whether or not they're true — is it useful, does it empower you"*, `4789IM-_-i4`) → counter-evidence → write the replacement → condition daily 30-60 days → **stack references** (*"they become like the legs to a tabletop which is your belief"*, `bDdDQeugO64`) → change the room.

### 5. Course-correction has no guidance, only labels
Our verdict taxonomy names outcomes but never helps choose one. His order of moves: change the approach → push the deadline 3-6 months → reshape the container → only then displace/drop. And he names the trap himself — changing a goal is also the dabbler's escape hatch — with the guard-rail question: **strategic reason, or lost excitement?** Six verdicts we lack: RESCHEDULED, PUSHED, RESHAPED, DISPLACED, PAUSED, DEFERRED-BY-CHOICE (*"I could have achieved it but I decided not to... it's okay if you don't do this"*, `zuEb-1Ll2h8`).

### 6. The bi-weekly after-action review
*"once you set the goals you got to check in every two weeks — are you on track, are you making progress, are you behind, what changes do you need to make"* — `GXhPOncX8CA`. Cleanest ready-made question set in the corpus; we have no equivalent cadence.

### 7. Numbers we were guessing at
- **15-20 goals** is his stated recommendation (`tYCT57Onfas`); 5 for a 100-day block.
- **80-90% hit rate** is his norm, stated in three videos — so a miss is expected, not failure.
- **+1 rule**: *"how can I bring myself to a three next week"* (`Kz83kMosOWU`) — never "get to 10".
- **Minimum time-in-grade** before a drop is allowed: ~6 months fitness, ~1 year business (`KopwaDbed4s`).

### 8. Single-person material (the Dating-area fix)
A 4-step approach-anxiety ladder (directions → the time → an attractive person's opinion → compliment/converse); the opener with its guardrail (*you walk away — "there's no rejection"*); rep targets in his words (10 approaches/day, 4 days/week); a **post-session journal** he actually kept (body language, eye contact, vocal tone, "what did I do that made her feel that way?"); reward the rep not the outcome; the partner-attraction exercise (journal exactly who you want + non-negotiables → *"who do I need to become to attract this?"*).

**Framing constraint:** he repudiates *The Game*'s method (negs, canned openers) while keeping the practice — *"I do not agree with or endorse it in any way"*. Product voice must be add-value / become-attractive, never "pickup".

### 9. Health and fitness were nearly empty despite being his pyramid's base
Now dense. Four versions of his morning ritual exist across eras; the definitive one (`PPlaK8y4PzA`) is read off a printed checklist. Highest-value gaps in our 14-step library:
1. **Smile** — his step #1 in 100% of versions, zero cost, we don't have it.
2. **The pre-moment ritual as an event-triggered category** — he says it now beats the morning one; our 6 categories are all time-of-day.
3. **Anchor the state** (fist / "yes") — the mechanic that makes every other emotional step compound.
4. **Duration tiers** — he ships 15/30/60/120-min versions of the same ritual and says outright he cycles 5-10 steps, not all of them. Our ritual is one fixed list.
5. **Stacking** — one step running underneath another (questions on the rebounder, meditation on the mat).
6. **A fasting/eating window** — the most load-bearing item in his health system (12-16h daily), and we have no habit shape for it.

**Wake time is NOT 5am** — inconsistent across eras (~8am no alarm in 2014; "usually 6:00" later). Don't ship a fixed wake time as his.

Steps of ours he never mentions: 10k steps, lay-out-clothes, nightly cleanup, write-200-words, low-screen evening. "Make your bed" appears only as a generic example, never his own practice.

### 10. Purpose is DECIDED, not discovered — and we have nothing for the common case
The biggest single reframe found. *"they feel like their purpose or their mission is this big mysterious thing that they take a lifetime to discover… and I say just pick something that you want your life to be about"* (`fICEjqpKfoY`); *"don't wait for what your calling is"* (`5ITfL1jNAsM`).

His procedure: release the expectation → journal your **criteria, not job titles** (his: freedom, difference, growth, money, creativity, travel) as a checklist you screen opportunities against → try many things, **one month minimum each**, journaling what you liked while doing it → re-evaluate annually → compress to a one-sentence mission with a BE and a DO/GIVE, said daily.

For someone with no idea at all: drop the word "passion" and ask *"what do you enjoy?"* — *"joy… that's a clue"*; the **"what lights you up when you talk about it"** test; and your current job **is** the mission or its stepping stone. Our product asks for a purpose statement and offers no route to one.

### 11. Language engineering
Rename each area to something that pulls — *"physical power — world class health and fitness"*, *"absolute financial freedom"*, *"spirit and soul — force for God"* (`8kco2rjijjE`). We support renaming; we never ask for it or explain why.

## Part 2 — His plan, as the worked example

**He reads his complete written vision aloud on camera in three separate videos, at three points in his life** — `8kco2rjijjE` (richest: full vision + purpose + identity + code of conduct + per-area paragraphs + his area renames), `OgRGJBpTOeU` (read off his closet doors), `PPlaK8y4PzA` (fullest and latest, read off the wall). Plus `9RxHchflvVs` "The Perfect Day" — an hour-by-hour first-person narrative covering every area, his most vivid vision content — and `I-SoCQvNi9A`, his honest **current-state scores** (family 6, spirituality 7, money 8, relationship 8.5; money was once a 2, mindset a 1).

That last one matters: it gives the exemplar a *from* as well as a *to*, which is exactly what a new user needs to see.

Our shipped example is 165 lines: "your 10" for 4 of 12 areas, per-area plans for 2, and 2 goals. **Replace it with the real thing.**

**Build `lifeMasteryExemplar.ts`** — all 12 areas populated: renamed area, his vision paragraph, his 0-10 score, purpose, identity line, goals with his actual `I will easily [X] by [date]` sentences and real outcomes (2016 revenue: missed by $40k on $2M; 198 of 200 videos, deliberately; *"massage 2×/month — I didn't achieve that goal either"*), values hierarchy, code of conduct, manifesto, morning ritual with durations, weekly/monthly rituals.

**Three honesty rules, or it's a lie:**
1. **Label the era.** Per-area paragraphs carry V1 numbers (190 lbs @ 8%, $2M/yr); the master document carries later ones (170 lbs @ 6%). Never silently mix.
2. **Mark reconstructions.** Mind/beliefs has no written vision paragraph anywhere — he added the area after the only version with per-area paragraphs. Reconstruct it, and say so on the card.
3. **Keep the misses.** The 80-90% hit rate is the most useful thing in it. An exemplar where everything landed teaches the wrong lesson.

## Part 3 — The guided build

A new mode beside Plan / Track / Library: **Guide** — his order of operations, one exercise at a time, each with the principle, his example, your input box, and a skip. Not a wizard that blocks the app; an optional rail you can leave and resume.

**Sessions** (his sequencing, his dosage — 30-60 min for goals, "a few hours" for vision/values):

| # | Session | What happens | His anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Get in state** | The physiology protocol, then a solitude/journal prompt. Gate: "ready" or "later". | *the first step* — `tYCT57Onfas` |
| 1 | **Debrief the year** | Good (first, no "but") → challenges → root cause → lessons | `zuEb-1Ll2h8` |
| 2 | **The whole-life vision** | Timed unfiltered write; present tense; the read-aloud goosebumps test | `8kco2rjijjE`, `PPlaK8y4PzA` |
| 3 | **Purpose, identity, code of conduct** | Three short exercises, in his order, straight after vision | `8kco2rjijjE` |
| 4 | **Derive your areas** | 7-12 from the vision, renamed to pull | `Kz83kMosOWU` |
| 5 | **Per area: your 10 → your score → your why → who you are here** | The existing room journey, re-ordered to match | `I-SoCQvNi9A` |
| 6 | **Brainstorm → number → circle** | 5-10 min timed dump per area, horizon-number each line (1/3/5/10/20), circle the one-year set, 80/20 twice | `tYCT57Onfas` |
| 7 | **Qualify each goal** | SMART sentence, belief ≥7 AND desire ≥7, why + pain-why, reasons drill, reward + consequence, pre-mortem | `GXhPOncX8CA` |
| 8 | **Chunk** | 1yr → 90-day → 30-day, with the worked arithmetic ($100k/yr → $8,400/mo) | `8kco2rjijjE` |
| 9 | **Rituals + balance the week** | Morning ritual, weekly/monthly rituals, daily budget | existing |
| 10 | **Commit** | Manifesto + sign → tracking | existing |

**Design rules:**
- **Every session shows his answer next to your box.** That's the whole point of the exemplar — "it's easier to write yours after reading one."
- **Resumable, never blocking.** A session is a card on the Guide rail; the normal flow stays fully usable.
- **Dosage honoured.** "If you can't handle five, start with one, start with two" (`GXhPOncX8CA`). Sessions 0-4 in one sitting is already a lot; the rail should say so and suggest stopping.
- **Belief AND desire, both ≥7** — we currently treat desire as optional.
- **Commit is a beginning.** He names set-and-forget as the biggest mistake people make; session 10 must hand over to the weekly loop, not end.

**Thin-area notes for session 5** (his own material is genuinely thin here, which is itself the finding): FUN is the area he *added* after realising he was winning at everything and not enjoying it — the prompt is "what did you do as a kid purely for fun?", with the escape-vs-addition guardrail. CONTRIBUTION has hard numbers (5-10% of income; 10% tithing while broke). SPIRITUALITY he deliberately under-teaches — *"I'm not here to convert you"* — so keep it practice-agnostic, as he does. FAMILY and FRIENDS are his thinnest: friends is all *selection* (the jealous-when-you-win filter), nothing on deepening.

**Then the loop** (post-commit, not part of the build): bi-weekly after-action check-in · the fractal review at every cadence (good → challenges → lessons → adjust) · course-correction with the strategic-vs-excitement guard-rail and the six new verdicts · the +1 rule on weak areas.

## v22 — THE FRONT DOOR (2026-07-30)

User verdict after all seven milestones: *"I asked you to completely look everything over, and I can't find a difference early on."* Correct, and measurable.

**The first viewport, measured:** 478px of chrome above the wheel in a 720px viewport — heading, subtitle, rail, a collapsed principle card, a three-line prose explainer, an example button, a get-in-state link. **The wheel was 62% visible; you could not see the hub.** And the Guide — the entire guided onboarding — was a 12px `#71717a` word in the header, visually indistinguishable from a static label.

Compare the first screen before and after the whole rebuild: heading → subtitle → a collapsed card → prose → half a wheel. **Identical shape.** Every milestone was scoped as "add a capability", so nothing was ever allowed to change the first screen. That is the mechanical reason a dozen rounds of work produced no visible difference early on: *rooms kept getting built and the front door was never in scope.*

**Fixed by subtraction and one routing decision:**
- **A blank slate now lands on the Guide.** It's what the guided path is for; it shouldn't be a footnote. Anyone with saved work resumes where they were.
- **The prose explainer above the wheel is deleted.** Three lines explaining a wheel you can't see is stale framing at its purest — it explains instead of showing.
- **The principle card, example button and get-in-state prompt moved BELOW the wheel.** The worked example kept its place (it's the pace-setter) — just not above the product.
- **Heading, subtitle and rail tightened.** Chrome above the wheel: **478px → 218px. The wheel is now 100% visible.**
- **The Guide pill is a real destination** (violet border/fill), and **Plan is always reachable** — it was gated on goals existing, which would have stranded a Guide-first user with no way to the wheel.
- **Clicking a room scrolls its panel into view.** It rendered 270px below the fold, so a wedge click looked like it did nothing.

**Three permanent guards added to `vision-plan-flow-audit.mjs`** so this class can't ship again:
1. `visibleCheck()` — asserts an element is **inside the viewport**, not merely present. The audit could only ever prove existence, which is how a panel 270px off screen passed every check.
2. **Screenshots default to VIEWPORT.** `fullPage` stitches the whole document into one image and therefore cannot show the fold — it is the exact tool that concealed this through a dozen "verified" passes. `shotFull()` exists for when the document's structure is the point.
3. Standing assertions: a blank slate lands on the Guide · the whole wheel fits the first viewport · clicking a room reveals its panel on screen.

**The rule this session earned:** *nothing gets added to that page without something being removed or collapsed.* Additive-only is why the front door never moved.

Verified: **1898 unit tests**, flow audit **35/35** (3 new v22 checks), typecheck clean, first viewport inspected as an image rather than a DOM query.
