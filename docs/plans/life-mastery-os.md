## Part 1 — The System As He Actually Teaches It (human explanation)

### The single most important structural finding
His system is a **strict derivation chain** — each layer is created FROM the one above, and the daily/weekly machinery exists to **re-read and re-feel** the upper layers, not just to track tasks:

```
ENTRY GATE (his Start Here step 1 + Date-With-Destiny lineage)
 0a. Commit to Mastery   dabbler vs master framing — commit to ALL areas, not one
 0b. Values elicitation  "What has been most important in my life? What else?" → rank
                         into a hierarchy of ≥7 values — done BEFORE vision, so vision
                         can be checked for value conflicts
      ▼
DRIVING FORCE (whole life, created next, in this order)
 1. Ultimate Vision      "If a magician could create the perfect life for you, what would it be?"
 2. Ultimate Purpose     ONE overarching, non-negotiable life-purpose statement
                         ("why do I want this vision? what will this give me?") —
                         per-area purposes exist in his fuller practice, but the master
                         purpose is the required one
 3. Identity             "Who am I committed to being?"  ("I am..." statements)
 4. Code of Conduct      standards / how I show up
      │  ("these four things are the driving force" — re-read WEEKLY, minimum)
      ▼
LIFE AREAS (his 8-12, each given a compelling custom name — "Physical Power")
 5. Per-area VISION      = "your 10" for that area ("if you don't know your 10, you can't measure")
 6. Per-area PURPOSE     why this area matters
 7. Per-area IDENTITY    role ("I'm an Adonis", "I'm a world-class coach")
      ▼
GOALS (per area; annual workshop + quarterly optional re-run)
 8. 5/10/20-yr goals     deliberately UNREALISTIC → vision board + vision chart (not SMART)
 9. 1-year goals         SMART sentence: "I will easily [X] at least [N] ([breakdown]) by [date]"
                         belief check ≥7/10 (below → shrink the goal) · result AND process types
10. Per-goal WHY         written DIRECTLY UNDER the goal ("reasons are the fuel") + pain-why
11. Could-do list → 80/20 → Massive Action Plan (incl. resources: coach, book, course, buddy)
12. Chunk down           1-yr → 90-day → 30-day sub-targets
      ▼
RITUALS & DAYS
13. Morning ritual       mind/body/spirit checklist; INCLUDES reading the driving force + goals
14. Daily top-3          from could-do lists, willpower-first ("eat the frog"), musts
15. Evening reflection   "3 amazing things today · how could today be better?"
```

**Where vision & why go (the user's question, answered from source):**
- **Vision sits at the very top, before areas and goals** — one master vision first, then a per-area vision which IS the "10" the weekly wheel rating measures against. We currently rate "vs your ideal" without ever capturing the ideal — that's the biggest gap in our lab.
- **The why attaches at EVERY level**: ultimate purpose (why of the vision) → per-area purpose → per-goal why (written under the goal, plus a pain-avoidance why) → per-weekly-outcome why. And the morning ritual re-reads them daily — "anytime you're not motivated, all you got to do is remind yourself: why do I want this?"

### The four operating rhythms (his cadences, verbatim-sourced)
- **DAILY**: morning ritual (smile→water→move+incantations→8 empowering questions→driving-force review→read 10 pages→plan day) · review goals + whys ("read 3×/day" ideal) · execute top 1-3 first thing · evening 2-question reflection.
- **WEEKLY** ("has to be a ritual"): re-read/associate to the whole plan · score every area 0-10 vs your 10 · journal reflection (magic moments, accomplishments, "what did I learn?") · capture everything for next week, categorize by area · set weekly outcomes per area WITH why · schedule blocks — "you have to do something each week to grow every area."
- **MONTHLY** (Goals Report; he later moved to quarterly — the durable element is *periodic public reporting*, which he credits with ~90% annual goal achievement, self-reported): per-goal actual vs target, delta vs last month, YTD vs annual, verdict (achieved / on-track / over-achieved / likely-miss / modified / cancelled / rescheduled — always with a reason), wheel re-rating with movement causes, then re-rank focus for next month.
- **ANNUAL** (+optional quarterly): **year-in-review FIRST** — (1) acknowledge successes, (2) review failures + top lessons — and only THEN the goal-setting workshop: peak state first, timed unlimited brainstorm, timeframe numbers (1/3/5/10/20), circle the 1-year goals, SMART-write them, why + pain-why, could-do → 80/20 → plan, **check every goal for alignment against the life plan/vision**, then make visible (wall/wallet) and declare publicly. His 100 Day Challenge layer (Gary Ryan Blair's external program he runs yearly): goals written once on day 1, then a *daily top-actions writing ritual* + weekly reviews — corroborates the daily top-3 + weekly evaluation design.

### Doctrines that shape UX (his words)
- "You can't manage what you don't measure" — measurement frequency determines failure granularity.
- "There's never a 10 — a 10 is when you stop growing" (re-baselining; his old 10 is today's 8).
- "Success is living at a 7+ in every area"; below 7 = weak spot → generate one small raise-the-score action.
- "Short-term goals attainable, long-term goals unrealistic"; belief must be ≥7/10 or shrink the goal.
- "Whatever gets rewarded gets repeated" — celebrate on-track check-ins explicitly.
- "Pressure creates diamonds" — public/buddy accountability with concrete stakes.
- "Balance doesn't exist — go for progress"; but touch every area weekly.
- Language engineering everywhere: compelling area names, "I will easily...", inspiring vision phrasing — re-reading IS the mechanism.

## Part 3 — The Redesigned Full Flow (build spec)

### Flow shape: three phases + four loops
**SETUP (once, guided):** Vision → Purpose → Identity (lite) → Areas & your-10s → Goals (AI-drafted from vision as today, then per-goal: SMART sentence, belief slider, why + pain-why) → Rituals & balance → Confirm.
**Track loops:** Daily (ritual, driving-force card, top-3, evening reflection) · Weekly (ratings vs your 10 + reflection + outcomes) · Monthly (report with verdicts + refocus) · The wheel/pyramid as standing dashboards.

### Milestones (each a testable user capability)
- **M0 Commit + Values**: a one-time "Commit to Mastery" gate (dabbler vs master framing, commit to all areas) followed by values elicitation — "what has been most important in your life?" free-add + drag-rank into a hierarchy (≥5); values stored in state, shown on the Driving Force card, and surfaced as a conflict check beside the vision ("does your vision honor your top values?"). (The app's existing `values`/`user_values` vocabulary can seed suggestions; sandbox keeps its own copy.)
- **M1 Driving Force**: guided Purpose builder after vision (reason-word chips + because-lines, ONE master purpose marked non-negotiable); Identity "I am..." list (optional); Track top card becomes a Driving Force card cycling values → vision → purpose → identity → a goal+why daily check ("I've connected to my driving force today").
- **M2 Your 10s**: per-Blueprint-area "what does your 10 look like?" short text (guided prompts, his per-area questions); shown beside weekly sliders + wheel tooltips; editable; "never a 10" re-baseline nudge when an area rates ≥9 two weeks running.
- **M3 Goal upgrade**: per-goal SMART sentence ("I will easily…" generated from title+measure+targetDate, editable); belief slider 0-10 with <7 → "shrink the goal" suggestion (chunk target down); pain-why field; result/process pairing hint per area.
- **M4 Weekly Evaluation v2**: adds 3 reflection prompts (magic moment / proudest accomplishment / lesson), weekly outcome+why for the chosen focus area(s) (max 3), "touch every area" indicator (which areas saw zero activity this week), below-7 areas → one-tap "raise-the-score action" pulled from routine library per area.
- **M5 Daily loop closure**: evening reflection (2 questions) stored per date; morning empowering-questions optional ritual step (guided 8 questions display); top-3 copy tightened ("first thing, willpower is highest").
- **M6 Monthly Report v2**: per-goal verdict (achieved/on-track/over-achieved/likely-miss/modified/cancelled/rescheduled) auto-suggested from pace + user-confirmable with reason; delta vs previous month; refocus step (re-rank priorities feeding the balancer); report includes weekly-reflection highlights. A "Year in review" variant renders the same report over the full year with successes-first-then-lessons ordering and a "re-run your goal setting" nudge.

Acceptance tests per milestone; all state extensions optional-field (`VisionPlanState`/`VisionProgress`) so existing sandboxes hydrate; all logic pure in `visionPlanService.ts`; no DB/RLS; LLM optional assists (SMART sentence polish, purpose suggestions) via existing fail-closed CLI routes only.

### Out of scope v1 (flagged for beta)
Vision board images · accountability buddy/public sharing (needs real users/DB) · annual workshop wizard (yearly nudge only) · incantation audio.

## v3 — THE LIFE PLAN (SHIPPED 2026-07-25, same day as OS v2)

The structural completion: Stefan's system is per-area EVERYTHING ("I have a vision and SMART goals for each area of my life"; 15-20 goals across all areas; compelling custom names per area; per-area purpose and identity). Until v3 the flow hung a few AI goals off one vision text — areas were tags, not the spine.

Built:
- **Third view: "Life Plan"** (3-tab header Plan / Life Plan / Track) — the area-by-area document. Overview strip ("the overlook"): 12 tiles with custom name, latest rating, goal count, 3 completeness dots (your-10 / why / goals). Then one card per area, foundation first: **compelling custom name** (EditableTitle; hint hides once renamed), **your 10** (same state as Plan's grid), **why this area matters**, **who you are here** (identity), **goals in this area** with type/belief/why chips, and an inline **AreaGoalComposer** (habit or milestone, days/week, measure, why, target date).
- **`VisionGoalDraft.areaId`** — Life Plan goals bind to ONE Blueprint area; `goalFeedsArea()` centralizes the rule (explicit area beats pillar mapping) and now drives blueprintCoverage, the pyramid inspector's Fed-by, and areasTouchedInWeek. `createAreaGoal()` (pure, fail-closed, collision-safe ids `lp-<area>-gN`) leaves `beliefLevel` null so the guided path immediately asks for calibration.
- **`VisionPlanState.areaPlans`** — per-area {name, purpose, identity}, optional, backward compatible.
- **Guided path extended**: "Set a goal in every area — N have none" and "Write the why for N more areas", both deep-linking into the Life Plan view (PendingAction.mode now includes "lifeplan").

Verified: 1714 unit tests green (v3 suites: goalFeedsArea scoping, createAreaGoal invariants, state round-trip, per-area actions), 16/16 Playwright checks (tab nav, overview, composer, no-leak scoping, badge navigation, Track integration, triple persistence), screenshot inspected.
