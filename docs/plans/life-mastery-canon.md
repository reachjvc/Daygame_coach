## THE COHERENT FRAME — Four Layers + a Toolkit

Everything Stefan James teaches snaps into four layers, each answering one question, each with its own cadence — plus a cross-cutting change toolkit deployed when measurement finds a stuck area. **This is the "overlook" the product should be organized around.**

```
①  IDENTITY   (who am I?)          — values · rules · identity · manifesto      [re-done yearly-ish]
②  DIRECTION  (what & why?)        — vision · your-10s/zeros · purpose · goals  [annual + quarterly]
③  ACTION     (what do I DO?)      — ritual matrix · morning/evening · RPM day  [daily/weekly]
④  MEASUREMENT (is it working?)    — day score · weekly wheel · reports · YIR   [daily→yearly, fractal]
⊕  TOOLKIT    (when stuck)         — belief swap · habit breaking · state change · rules repair
```

The loop: ④ finds a weak spot → ⊕ intervenes or ③ adds a ritual → ② adjusts goals at the next review → ① re-ranks values every year or two. "You can't manage what you don't measure" is the engine; "whatever gets rewarded gets repeated" is the fuel.

## ① IDENTITY LAYER

**Values (complete procedure — Lp_GOrM16Xc, _Axwu-OV9YQ; Robbins DWD/Demartini lineage; redo every 1-2 years):**
1. *Current audit*: "What's been most important to me in my life?" → "What else?" loop; first answer is in the gut. Convert means→ends (family→love? money→freedom?) — a value IS an emotion.
2. *Rank pairwise*: "What's been more important — this one or this one?" ≥7 values (that's a count, not a rating).
3. **Away-from values** (NEW): "What emotional states am I trying to avoid?" — rejection, failure, shame… ranked too; the #1 away-from "shapes decisions more than anything."
4. *Conflict audit*: read consequences off the ordering (security>success = never risks; success>happiness = can't happily achieve; fitness>health = his shot adrenals).
5. **Re-design from vision** (NEW — corrects our order-of-operations): after vision exists, ask "What do my values NEED to be to create the life I want?" — add/remove/re-rank. So: audit → vision → re-design, values bracket the vision.
6. *Condition*: wall/daily read; live them in order inside the morning ritual.

**Rules engineering (NEW, big):** every value/emotion has rules ("to feel successful, ___ must happen"). Rewrite rules to be *easy to feel good, hard to feel bad*: "I feel successful the moment I wake up"; "To feel rejected I'd have to value their opinion above my own."
