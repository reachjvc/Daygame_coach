## The name: where it stands (2026-10-16)

Nothing is decided. What the owner said:

  The channel and the business can share the name or not.
- He wants a name that is very appealing to him, memorable, and says mastery,
  success, progress and fully getting the life you want. Dating first, the whole

## Claude's notes

- **The data on naming in this market** (from the research corpus, 2026-10-16):
  33 of the 50 in-niche YouTube channels with 1M+ subscribers use a person's
  name. Six use a plain promise phrase (Better Than Yesterday, Charisma on
  Command). None use a Latin or metaphor word. Big creators split the names:
  Ali Abdaal is the channel, Superfocus the app, LifeOS the course,
  Feel-Good Productivity the book. That points to Vindahl for the channel and
  author, plus a separate app name.
- **NextRep and Reps are crowded.** On 2026-10-16 the App Store had 16 apps
  named NextRep or Next Rep (almost all fitness, one from NEXTREP, L.L.C.) and
  19 named Reps. nextrep.com, nextrep.app and @nextrep on YouTube are taken.
  trynextrep.com and usenextrep.com were free.
- **"Rep" forms that were free everywhere on 2026-10-16** (.com, .app, YouTube
  handle, no App Store app with the name): IRL Reps (irlreps), Ten Thousand
  Reps (tenthousandreps), 10K Reps (tenkreps; 10kreps.com was taken).
- **The recipe behind the names he admires:** two everyday words with one
  tension (Modern Wisdom, Atomic Habits), a familiar frame holding a big subject
  (The Art of, School of, Order of, a name + Lab), or a plain promise (Better
  Than Yesterday, Escaping Ordinary).
- **Names built from that recipe, checked 2026-10-16** (no App Store app with
  the name for any of them):
  - Free on .com, .app and the YouTube handle: Winning Slowly, Art of Going
    First, Ten Year Man, Vindahl Lab.
  - Free on .com and .app, YouTube handle taken: Future Proud, Your Best Decade.
  - Free on .app and YouTube, .com taken: Best Decade, Quiet Confidence, School
    of Courage, Brave Habits, A Life in Full, Ten Good Years.
  - Free on .app only: Man in Progress, Hard Earned, Going First, Everyday
    Mastery.
  - Web searches found no brand using Winning Slowly or Future Proud.
- **The owner's two suggestions, checked 2026-10-16:**
  - Perfect Practice: perfectpractice.com sells golf training equipment, and
    the App Store has Perfect Practice (music), Perfect Practice Sports and a
    golf app. perfectpracticeapp.com was free on .com, .app and YouTube.
  - Perfectable: no App Store app, and perfectable.app was free. But
    perfectable.com is a company "creating technology that empowers people to
    improve their lives", the same space, and @perfectable on YouTube was
    taken. The dictionary spelling is "perfectible".
- **Round 4 (2026-10-16):** 146 names in the zone he reacted to (reps, practice,
  the ten-year game, the man in progress), each checked on .com with get-/the-/
  -app variants, .app, YouTube and the App Store, are on the naming board at
  https://claude.ai/artifact/DSD8J1PJt4533EPytGD9v4. His Yes/Maybe/No marks are
  kept in its `marks` collection and his notes in `notes/owner`. Read them with
  the ArtifactData tool before the next round.
- **10kreps.com is someone else's app in the making (checked 2026-10-16).**
  Registered 2025-12-29 at Namecheap, expires 2026-12-29, behind Cloudflare. It
  serves an Expo (React Native) web build titled "10K Reps" with Google sign-in
  and a Supabase backend. It is not on the App Store and web searches found
  nothing about it. tenkreps.com, tenkreps.app, @tenkreps and the
  tenthousandreps forms were free.
- **A product form of the campaign line, not yet asked for:** let a user write
  the sentence he wants to be able to say, and bring it back to him after 1, 5
  and 10 years. The audience already does this by hand ("I'll be back in a year
