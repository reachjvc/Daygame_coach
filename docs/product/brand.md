# Brand, name and campaign ideas

**The owner's choices and the lines he liked, kept so they surface when launch,
marketing, the landing page, the book or the YouTube channel are worked on.**
`docs/product/vision.md` says what the product is for; this file says what it is
called and how it is promoted.

Rules for this file:

- The owner's items are his words. Claude adds an item only when the owner said it
  in a session, with the date, and never rewords or drops one.
- Claude's own notes go under "Claude's notes", marked as such.
- Availability was true on the date given. Check again before buying anything.

## The name: where it stands (2026-10-16)

Nothing is decided. What the owner said:

- He likes his surname, **Vindahl**, and is fine with the brand pointing to it.
  The channel and the business can share the name or not.
- He wants a name that is very appealing to him, memorable, and says mastery,
  success, progress and fully getting the life you want. Dating first, the whole
  life later.
- **Liked:** Reps, and NextRep even more. Ten Thousand Reps "could work"
  (2026-10-16).
- **Names he admires:** Modern Wisdom, Art of Accomplishment.
- **His own suggestions:** Perfect Practice, Perfectable, Better Man (2026-10-16).
- **The plain .com must be free** (2026-10-16): he does not want to look through
  names whose .com is taken, and get-/-app forms do not count.
- **Thinking in buckets** (sorting ideas into families) did not work for him; he
  asked for more creative methods.
- **Round 5 over-indexed on a few words** (reps, ten, years, man, practice),
  because every writer was shown his liked names. He asked for inspiration from
  further afield (2026-10-16).
- **He loves Dollar Shave Club as a name**, and wants the domain to look good
  written out in one string (2026-10-16).
- **He suggested studying the best names in history** (Apple and the like),
  noting a name can be somewhat random and still great.
- **Marked Yes on the board:** 10K Reps, Thousand Reps. **Maybe:** Man in
  Progress, Ten Year Man.
- **Rejected:** Frontfoot ("not appealing at all"), Vindrose ("super bad"), and
  the whole style of Latin mottos, Danish words and nautical metaphors.

## Claude's notes

- **The data on naming in this market** (from the research corpus, 2026-10-16):
  33 of the 50 in-niche YouTube channels with 1M+ subscribers use a person's
  name. Six use a plain promise phrase (Better Than Yesterday, Charisma on
  Command). None use a Latin or metaphor word. Big creators split the names:
  Ali Abdaal is the channel, Superfocus the app, LifeOS the course,
  Feel-Good Productivity the book. That points to Vindahl for the channel and
  author, plus a separate app name.
- **NextRep and Reps are crowded.** On 2026-10-16 the App Store had 16 apps
  named NextRep or Next Rep (almost all fitness, one from NEXTREP, L.L.C.) and
  19 named Reps. nextrep.com, nextrep.app and @nextrep on YouTube are taken.
  trynextrep.com and usenextrep.com were free.
- **"Rep" forms that were free everywhere on 2026-10-16** (.com, .app, YouTube
  handle, no App Store app with the name): IRL Reps (irlreps), Ten Thousand
  Reps (tenthousandreps), 10K Reps (tenkreps; 10kreps.com was taken).
- **The recipe behind the names he admires:** two everyday words with one
  tension (Modern Wisdom, Atomic Habits), a familiar frame holding a big subject
  (The Art of, School of, Order of, a name + Lab), or a plain promise (Better
  Than Yesterday, Escaping Ordinary).
- **Names built from that recipe, checked 2026-10-16** (no App Store app with
  the name for any of them):
  - Free on .com, .app and the YouTube handle: Winning Slowly, Art of Going
    First, Ten Year Man, Vindahl Lab.
  - Free on .com and .app, YouTube handle taken: Future Proud, Your Best Decade.
  - Free on .app and YouTube, .com taken: Best Decade, Quiet Confidence, School
    of Courage, Brave Habits, A Life in Full, Ten Good Years.
  - Free on .app only: Man in Progress, Hard Earned, Going First, Everyday
    Mastery.
  - Web searches found no brand using Winning Slowly or Future Proud.
- **The owner's two suggestions, checked 2026-10-16:**
  - Perfect Practice: perfectpractice.com sells golf training equipment, and
    the App Store has Perfect Practice (music), Perfect Practice Sports and a
    golf app. perfectpracticeapp.com was free on .com, .app and YouTube.
  - Perfectable: no App Store app, and perfectable.app was free. But
    perfectable.com is a company "creating technology that empowers people to
    improve their lives", the same space, and @perfectable on YouTube was
    taken. The dictionary spelling is "perfectible".
- **Round 4 (2026-10-16):** 146 names in the zone he reacted to (reps, practice,
  the ten-year game, the man in progress), each checked on .com with get-/the-/
  -app variants, .app, YouTube and the App Store, are on the naming board at
  https://claude.ai/artifact/DSD8J1PJt4533EPytGD9v4. His Yes/Maybe/No marks are
  kept in its `marks` collection and his notes in `notes/owner`. Read them with
  the ArtifactData tool before the next round.
- **10kreps.com is someone else's app in the making (checked 2026-10-16).**
  Registered 2025-12-29 at Namecheap, expires 2026-12-29, behind Cloudflare. It
  serves an Expo (React Native) web build titled "10K Reps" with Google sign-in
  and a Supabase backend. It is not on the App Store and web searches found
  nothing about it. tenkreps.com, tenkreps.app, @tenkreps and the
  tenthousandreps forms were free.
- **Better Man is taken in his exact space** (checked 2026-10-16): betterman.com
  is a Christian men's ministry running since 1999, betterman.app is taken, the
  App Store already has "Better Man: Self-Improvement", "A Better Man Plan" and
  others, and the 2024 Robbie Williams film owns search.
- **Round 5 (2026-10-16), no buckets:** five writers each worked one way: a
  brand copywriter from a manifesto, a screenwriter from one man's ten-year
  story, an internet-native creator from imagined top comments, a podcast host
  from the sound of Modern Wisdom and Order of Man, and one from his own words in
  vision.md and the concept docs. Of about 145 names, 18 had a free plain .com.
  Claude added 15 more built to keep a free .com while reading naturally (Reps
  Make the Man, Practice Makes Him, A Decade of Reps, The Ten Year Man). The
  board now shows only these and the 31 round-4 names with a free plain .com,
  59 in all.
- **Lessons from round 5:** a free plain .com almost always needs three or more
  words, a number, or a coined word. One- and two-word English phrases were
  taken in about 9 cases out of 10. In this niche "a ten" means an attractive
  woman, so a bare "ten" name can misread; "ten years" and "10K reps" do not.
- **Round 6 (2026-10-16):** no example names were shown to the writers, and the
  round-5 words were banned. Nine writers each drew from one source: literature,
  music and film, science and the body, crafts and sport, everyday moments,
  invented words, the mechanisms behind history's best brand names, arbitrary
  Apple-style words, and invented characters and clubs. About 540 names in
  total; 100 had a free plain .com and went on the board. Free-.com rate by
  source: invented characters and clubs 29 of 59, literature 21 of 60,
  everyday moments 16 of 60, crafts and sport 12 of 60, music and film 9 of
  60, science 8 of 60, great-brand patterns 3 of 54, arbitrary pairings 2 of
  70, invented short words 0 of about 108 (couragent.com, for example, has been
  registered since 2005).
- **Domain readability:** the board flags a domain that hides a bad word when
  the words run together (expertsexchange, therapistfinder) or runs past 20
  letters. The rule lives in the board's build script, not in this repo.
- **A product form of the campaign line, not yet asked for:** let a user write
  the sentence he wants to be able to say, and bring it back to him after 1, 5
  and 10 years. The audience already does this by hand ("I'll be back in a year
  to see what's changed", 4,200 likes).
