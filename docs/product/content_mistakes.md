# Content mistakes

One entry per thing that went wrong in content work: the date, what
happened, the cause and what changed in the process. Newest first. Started
2026-11-21 (Claude, proposed in `Plans/outvoted-animation-round.md`, Part B).

His exact words are in `content_creation_history.md`; the lessons are in
`content_creation_process.md`. This file only records. Tracks: Track 1 =
celebrity clip reels; Track 2 = our own films; Video 01; Look = finding the
look.

Not in this file: quotes from forums, names of private people, celebrity
names, video ids, health details. A celebrity reel is "a celebrity quit
reel". Details stay in the private folders an entry points to.

## How this file is kept (Claude's proposal, not his rule)

- Append only.
- When a later entry overrules an earlier one, the earlier one gets
  "(superseded by Hn)".
- A content session reads the last 10 entries before starting work, after
  the two content docs.
- The file records things. It does not make new rules: "What changed" only
  cites his words or says "proposed" or "nothing yet".
