# The vice module — the owner's concept

**The owner's own words, from 2026-09-19, split into numbered items by Claude and
left in the owner's voice.** `docs/product/vision.md` is the whole product's
destination; this is the concept for one slice.

Rules for this file, same as the vision:

- The owner adds and corrects items. Claude condenses only when asked, and never
  invents an item or quietly drops one.
- Never renumber. Plans and replies refer to items by number.
- A plan under `docs/plans/vice-*.md` must answer every item by number.

## The concept

1. **People relapse in various ways, and the ways repeat.** The tool should show
   the different times I quit and the reasons I stopped being sober, so that it
   accumulates and we don't make the same mistake twice.

2. **Close calls, from aviation safety.** I like the idea of logging the moments
   that did not become a relapse, and I like that the framing comes from how
   aviation treats near misses.

3. **The important periods of quitting, understood properly** — what I did to get
   it going, what kept it underway, and what ended it.

4. **The purpose is to be read, at one specific moment.** This understanding is
   part of my consciousness when I reject my own idea to moderate. The tool
   exists to be in my head when the thought "I could moderate" arrives.

5. **Both, in combination.** Close calls and the history of attempts are one
   tool, not two.

6. **It has to look genuinely good.** I want it shown beautifully.

7. **What is there now is more or less useless.** Use the existing material or
   the research to bolster the new thing, but be careful: AI wrote the rules for
   vices, so they are not necessarily my real opinion.

8. **Clicking Vices shows the new work in isolation.** I should not have to click
   around old work to reach it.

9. **It does not have to write to the website yet** — unless a real programmer
   would make everything deployable from the start, before the final shape of a
   tool is known.

## How to read item 7

The distinction the plan draws, for checking later: a **research finding** with a
source outside this repo (Marlatt, Grubbs, West & Sohal, NSDUH, the 15-source
testimonial corpus) is evidence and survives. A **product decision** AI made —
six flows, three copy versions, the learn spine, the shortlist, the hub grouping
— is taste, and does not. Item 7 is read as: keep what has a citation, drop what
has an opinion.
