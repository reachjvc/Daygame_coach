# The audience in its own words

**1,822 top-ranked comments** across 91 videos, representing **4.75M likes**, after filtering a book-promo bot ring (38 comments, 14,810 bought likes).

Comments are sorted by likes within each video, so these are the lines the audience itself voted up — not a sample I chose.

**What this file is for, and what it is not.** A like count measures how hard a line landed inside one video's audience. That makes this an excellent source of *phrasing* and a poor source of *findings* — of the 100 most-liked comments in the corpus, zero report anything that actually happened. For evidence of what is true across the genre, use the convergence ranking in `00-synthesis.md` instead, which counts how many unconnected channels show the same thing.

Use them as the source of phrasing for any hook, headline or onboarding question: **write in these words, not in the creators' words.**

Quotes are verbatim, including typos.
