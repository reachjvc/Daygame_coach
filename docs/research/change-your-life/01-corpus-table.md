## How to read the columns

- **Tier / mult** — this video's views-per-day divided by the median views-per-day of *the same channel's other uploads from the same era* (within half to double this video's age, ≥6 peers required). It answers: did this topic outperform what this creator normally does? Raw views only tell you the channel is big.
- The ratio inflates on small channels, where peer videos have few views — so the **tier is the headline and the number is supporting detail**.
- **v/sub** — views per subscriber. Age-independent reach relative to audience size.
- **like %** and **cmt %** — engagement rates. Age- and size-independent; these measure resonance rather than algorithmic distribution. Corpus median like-rate is 3.62%.
- **no baseline** = fewer than 6 same-era peers existed in the scraped history, so no honest comparison was possible. No number is shown rather than a fake one.

**Selection bias, stated plainly:** videos were chosen for high view counts, so this corpus studies *what worked*. It is not a controlled sample — the near-absence of underperformers is a property of the selection, not a finding.
