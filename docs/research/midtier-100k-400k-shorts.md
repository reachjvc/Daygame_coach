# Mid-Tier (100k–400k) Self-Help / Psychology / Philosophy / Business — Shorts Analysis

**Method:** YouTube channel-search (filtered to channels) to discover candidates in the 100k–400k band, then live scrape of each channel's `/shorts` tab sorted by "Popular" (view-ranked). Subs + Short views read live **2026-06-17**.

**Cohort:** 25 channels, representative mix of *genuine creators* and *faceless content farms*, spread across the niche. Labeled by type.

## Key findings — what wins in Shorts at this tier

1. **Subs ≠ Shorts views, even more than at the top.** Many 200–370k faceless channels top out at 5–90k on their *best* Short. Their sub counts come from long-form or are inflated — not from Shorts.
2. **The mid-tier outliers are NOT philosophy or quotes — they're curiosity / health / body / dating hooks.** The single biggest Short in the whole cohort: Stoic Post (216k subs) "Why Older People Wake Up At 3 AM" = **5.4M**. Same channel: "Why Some People Look Younger Than Their Age" 3.1M, "Most Attractive Traits In A Man" 1.6M.
3. **Winning formats:**
   - Curiosity gap + body/health ("Why older people wake at 3am", "Why some look younger")
   - Numbered listicles ("5 Things Men Should Never Do With Women", "7 Signs…")
   - Dating / attraction / relationship hooks (esp. for-men)
   - Dark-psych / manipulation / boundaries ("5 boundaries that make you impossible to manipulate")
   - Actionable mental-health technique (CBT/DBT) — Self-Help Toons "Relieve Depression With This Simple Step" 954k
   - **Mini-story / character** ("Diogenes, the craziest philosopher" tops two different channels) — story beats abstract, matching the big-creator pattern.
4. **What underperforms / is absent:** abstract philosophy explainers, generic stoic quote cards, academic lectures, podcast clips without a hook. Genuine long-form philosophy/psychology creators **largely don't post Shorts at all** (Orion Philosophy, Let's Talk Psychology, Stoic Training, Productivity Guy = 0 Shorts; Desi Philosopher, Psychology Simplified = 1–3).
5. **Story-driven > abstract** is confirmed at this tier too. The narrative/character Shorts (Diogenes anecdotes) and the curiosity/body-mystery Shorts massively outperform quote cards on the same channels.
