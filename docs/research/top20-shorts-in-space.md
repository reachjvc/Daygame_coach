# 20 Popular Shorts in the Self-Help / Psychology / Philosophy / Business Space — Discovered Blind (regardless of creator)

**Method:** YouTube search filtered to **Shorts only**, across topic queries (stoicism, self improvement, psychology facts, motivation). Search-result view counts proved unreliable (parse artifacts — e.g. one showed "102M" for a Short that is actually 2.46M), so **every Short below was opened individually and its true `viewCount` + channel read from the watch page.** Verified live 2026-06-18.

**Caveat:** This is a blind sweep of a handful of topic queries, not a provably-exhaustive top 20 of the entire space. Bigger Shorts exist (the famous giants' Shorts — e.g. GaryVee 41M — outrank several of these). What this captures is *which channels surface and win when you search the space's core topics* — i.e. who actually owns Shorts real-estate here.

## Headline finding: the views do NOT go to the famous creators

Of the top 20, **only one is a household-name author** (Mark Manson — and his sits near the *bottom* at 2.46M). The rest are:
- **Motivation compilation/aggregator channels** repackaging others' speeches (Goggins, interviews) with cinematic editing + music — Bolt Motivation (2 entries inc. #1 at 29.4M), Ben Lionel Scott, B-UNSTOPPABLE, Motivation_error2.0, success_affection.
- **Dark / pop-psychology "facts" farms** (text-on-screen) — UpgradeYourSelf_Knowledge (2 entries), astheticavi, relationshipsfactsz, rshmindtree.
- **Stoic quote farms** — StoicismLegion (7.9M, a 100K-sub channel from the mid-tier study), Stoic-w8o.
- **Aesthetic "daily reminder / quotes" channels** — DailyReminderr1, motivationlife.
- **Clip channels of big names** — jordanpetersonrulesforlife (JBP clips; the *channel* isn't Peterson).
- **A couple of genuine self-improvement creators** — growingannanas (15.7M), Tom Beckles (4.46M), Mark Manson (2.46M), Pritika Loonia.

**So "who gets Shorts views" in this space = format-first faceless/aggregator channels, frequently repackaging the famous creators' own material.** The creators make the ideas; the farms get the Shorts views.

## What the winning Shorts have in common (content patterns)
1. **Repackaged motivational speech** + cinematic edit + trending/epic music (Goggins, "GET UP AND GRIND").
2. **"Facts" listicles** — dark psychology, attraction, "facts about girls/guys."
3. **Numbered self-improvement listicles** — "7 habits", "6 habits of high IQ", "5 steps to glow up."
4. **Identity / relationship / gender hooks** — "...as a man", "when a guy is in love", "respect yourself", "high IQ."
5. **Curiosity + number + identity** is the dominant title formula.
6. **Street-interview "what's some good advice?"** format (Bolt's #1).

This is the inverse of the giants, who win Shorts on **personality + story** (DOAC's plane story, GaryVee's Hot Wheels). In blind discovery, **format and packaging beat fame.**
