# Mid-Tier (100k–500k) Self-Improvement Channels — Outperforming Videos

**Method:** Discover channels in 100–500k via YouTube channel-search; for each, scrape the `/videos` tab — recent uploads = **baseline (median views)**, "Popular" sort = **top videos**. An "outperformer" = views well above the channel's baseline (multiple shown as ×). Verified live 2026-06-24.

**Two caveats up front:**
1. **Baseline is distorted for declining channels.** Many of these channels' recent uploads get a fraction of their old hits, so the × multiple is inflated when a channel has faded. Read the **absolute views + age** as the real signal; × is context.
2. **The biggest hits are usually 1–3 years OLD** — the videos that *grew* the channel. Recent breakouts are rarer (flagged where they occur). For a newcomer this is the key lesson: a channel is usually defined by a handful of breakout ideas, not consistent output.

## Important finding for the brand question
The 100–500k self-improvement band is **dominated by faceless farms, non-English (Hindi/Punjabi/Amharic), faith-motivation, and manosphere channels.** Genuine English person-led self-improvement creators are *scarce* here — most are either under 100k or have already grown past 500k (Clark Kegley 1.94M, etc.). **That scarcity is white space**: a genuine English personal brand in this niche has less direct mid-tier competition than the sheer volume of channels suggests.

## What overperforms — the patterns (the actual deliverable)
1. **"I tried X for N days" experiments** — Science of Self-Care: *20,000 steps/day for 30 days* 4.2M; weighted vest 636K; LED mask 927 days 635K. Personal experiment + verdict.
2. **Skill-listicle + identity/number** — *How to talk to anyone / communication skills* (FOCUS STUDY 3.2M; Clark Kegley "social skills that make you attractive" 3.4M), *body language for men* (ReBorn 2.4M), *dark psychology tricks* (ReBorn 2.5M).
3. **Relatable hard-truths** — Masood: *Your Boss Is Not Your Friend* 1.1M, *You are not your job* 926K. Anti-work / identity truths.
4. **Study & discipline for young people** — Ruff: *How I only study 2 hours a day* 1.2M, *make studying FUN like a video game* 1.3M, *If you're 13–18 watch this* 1.1M, *stop scrolling* 521K. Age-targeted + relatable.
5. **Evergreen actionable mental-health technique** — Self-Help Toons (CBT/DBT/ACT, all 250–384K, 5–6 yrs old, still top); Glow Up Secrets (attachment/nervous-system). Searchable, compounds over years.
6. **Curiosity + body/health** — *what happens when you…*; weed-withdrawal "levels" (Addiction Mindset).
7. **Contrarian / topical take on an evergreen theme** — Medieval Mindset: *Gen Z Culture Is Basically Medieval China* 2.1M; *Why Medieval Knights Went Insane* 2.3M (a **3-week-old** recent breakout — rare).
8. **Borrowed authority / celebrity** — Upgrade Your Mindset: *Michael Caine reads "If"* 10M, *Morgan Freeman reads Invictus* 1.4M.
9. **Niche-down wins big** — Addiction Mindset (just *quitting weed*), Glow Up Secrets (just *anxious attachment*) — narrow niche → outsized multiples + loyal audience.

**Meta-lesson:** multiples of 10–200× are common because baselines are low. **One right idea/title massively outperforms a channel's norm.** You don't need consistency to break out — you need to hit the right format, then double down. The list above is the proven format menu.
