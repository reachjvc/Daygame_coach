TRAJECTORY SCORE — Read her response patterns across ALL turns:

Your primary signal is what SHE does, not what technique HE uses. Read every one of her responses in sequence and assess the pattern.

WARMING signals (trajectory rises):
- Her answers get longer over time
- She volunteers personal details unprompted
- She asks him questions back
- She laughs or makes jokes ("Haha", playfulness)
- She self-corrects or elaborates ("Nej... jo, actually...")
- She seeks his validation ("It's cute though, right?")
- She shares experiences, photos, stories

COOLING signals (trajectory drops):
- Her answers get shorter over time
- She deflects or gives flat responses ("Okay", "Hmm", monosyllables)
- She mentions needing to leave ("Jeg skal videre", "I have to go")
- She corrects him without adding anything new
- She brings up boyfriend or excuse to leave
- She stops engaging with his frame

STALLING signals (trajectory flat):
- She answers politely but never invests (consistent short answers)
- She doesn't ask back or share details for 3+ turns
- She stays on neutral topics
- She's present but not warming

Trajectory scale:
- 1-3: Cold — checking out (short answers, deflections, exit signals)
- 4-5: Guarded — polite but not investing (answering but flat)
- 6-7: Curious — engaged (longer answers, details, questions back)
- 8-10: Interested — investing heavily (flirting, sharing freely, seeking his attention)

LINE SCORE — Context-aware quality of his latest move:

Score his line based on what it achieves IN THIS CONVERSATION, not in isolation.

A line that:
- Continues a coherent thread she's engaging with → +1-2 above default
- Pivots smoothly after she deflected → +1 (shows calibration)
- Opens something she can invest in (hook, tease, cold read) → +1
- Is the 3rd+ question in a row with no statements/teases → -1 per consecutive question (interview mode)
- Repeats something she already shut down → -2 (tone deaf)
- Ignores what she just said → -1 (not listening)
- Is safe but adds nothing new → default score

Temperature-based defaults (what a "just okay" line scores at each trajectory level):
- At trajectory 1-3 (cold): default 4. Only humor or strong hooks reach 7.
- At trajectory 4-5 (guarded): default 5. Good threading or engagement-building reach 7.
- At trajectory 6-7 (curious): default 6. She's engaged — keeping it going = 6. Building energy = 7.
- At trajectory 8+ (interested): default 6. She's invested — almost anything reasonable = 6.

Score ABOVE default when his line creates energy, threads well, or opens something interesting.
Score BELOW default when his line kills momentum, repeats patterns, or ignores her signals.

PATTERNS TO RECOGNIZE:

INTERVIEW MODE: 3+ questions in a row without statements, teases, or cold reads. Penalize line_score by -1 for each consecutive question past the 2nd, regardless of question quality.

THREAD BUILDING: He references something from 2+ turns ago, creating continuity. Reward +1 — shows listening and building, not just reacting turn by turn.

JOKE RECYCLING: He repeats or extends something she already shut down or corrected. Penalty -2 — shows he's not adapting to her signals.

PIVOT AFTER DEFLECTION: She deflects and he smoothly changes topic instead of pressing. Reward +1 — shows social calibration.

STATEMENT/QUESTION RATIO: If his last 3+ turns were all questions, the next question scores lower even if it's individually decent. Healthy conversations mix statements, teases, and questions.
